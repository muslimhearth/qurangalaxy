# G11: The galaxy forms with a hint of «القرآن الكريم»

## Done
- **Intro on first load** (galaxy first, words second, per the user's direction change):
  1. *Appear* (1.6 s): the root stars light up from right to left. Each star leans part of the way toward a point inside the words. A faint calligraphy of pale-gold specks lights up in the same wave.
  2. *Hold* (2.5 s): the stars twinkle gently. The words read on a second look, inside a still clearly visible galaxy.
  3. *Morph* (3.2 s): the stars ease (cubic in-out) to their real places with a right-to-left wave (up to 1.1 s delay, the Arabic reading direction). The specks drift apart and fade.
  4. *Done*: links fade in (1.5 s), labels appear as before, picking and hover start, and the idle rotation eases in.
- **Words**: «ٱلْقُرْءَانُ ٱلْكَرِيمُ» in Amiri Quran. The browser shapes the text on an offscreen canvas after `document.fonts.load`. Desktop uses one line. Phones (aspect < 0.9) stack the two words.
- **Pure modules with tests** (`src/scene/intro/`):
  - `sample.ts`: Poisson-like dart throwing inside the opaque pixels of an ImageData-like input. Gives exactly N points. Deterministic.
  - `assign.ts`: band matching. Both point sets are cut into equal-count y bands and matched left to right in each band. Gives a deterministic permutation that keeps the screen layout, so no long crossing paths.
  - `timeline.ts`: phase lengths, easing, per-star delay, appear and morph progress, pull per star size, twinkle, skip rules, `IntroClock` (skip, finish, freeze) and `introPhase`.
  - `frame.ts`: the text plane that faces the start camera, and the 3D placement with depth jitter.
  - `plan.ts`: the star plan (target, pull, delay, phase), `writeIntroMatrices`, and the dust plan.
  - `phrase.ts`: the words and the line layout.
- **Rendering** (no React re-render per frame):
  - `IntroDriver` loads the font, samples, plans and advances the clock.
  - `Stars` writes the instance matrices in `useFrame` until the intro ends, then writes the galaxy matrices once.
  - `IntroDust`: one instanced `Sprite` (14,000 specks, 10,000 on phones). The GPU animates it from one time uniform. It unmounts at the end.
  - `Links` stays hidden, then fades in. `LabelTracker` keeps the labels hidden until the end.
- **Skip rules**:
  - The intro does not play with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, with a `#/root/…` deep link or with `?nointro`.
  - A pointer down, key press or wheel event during the intro (capture phase on `window`) jumps to the morph and plays it in about 1 s. A selection (search) does the same.
  - If the font does not load within 3 s, the galaxy shows at once.
- **Automated browsers** (`navigator.webdriver`) skip the intro unless the URL has `?intro`. The existing e2e tests therefore run unchanged.
- **`?introAt=<s>`** freezes the intro at that time, until any input. Use it for reliable screenshots and for tuning.
- **Body attribute**: `<body data-intro>` holds `appear | words | morph | done`. It is written only when the phase changes. Tests poll it.
- **Browser test** `e2e/intro.spec.ts` (desktop and android) checks:
  - The frozen words screenshot has lit pixels and no labels.
  - A tap or click finishes the intro.
  - The intro plays through on its own, and labels show only after it.
  - A deep link skips the intro.
  - Reduced motion skips the intro.

## Decisions
- **Subtle pull, not crisp text.** Each star moves 20–55 % of the way to its glyph point. Big stars move least and stay as landmarks. The readable shape comes from the faint dust, so the scene stays a galaxy.
- **Clock on wall time** with steps capped at 0.5 s. The first two frames after the plan is ready do not count (shader compile). Slow software renderers then still finish in seconds.
- **The intro state is local to the scene** (a mutable object passed as a prop). There is no store change.
- **The idle rotation holds still** during the intro, so the words face the camera. `CameraRig` now uses the shared `START_ELEVATION` and `START_FIT`.
- **No diacritic special-casing.** The harakat in the dust read well at 14,000 specks.

## Open issues
- **No "replay" control.** Replay needs a camera reset to the start pose and a fresh intro state. Left out to keep the change small.
- **Port 5194 conflict.** Port 5194 was in use by another worktree's preview server, so I ran the e2e tests on `E2E_PORT=5212`.
- **Load-related flakes.** Under heavy machine load (load average about 25), `app.spec.ts` "loads the real galaxy" sometimes timed out waiting for the canvas (5 s). It passed on rerun. This is not related to the intro.
- **WebKit (`iphone`) not run.** It needs the container.
