# G8: English meanings

## Done
- Every root and lemma in `public/data/galaxy.json` has `en`: short English meanings, best first. 1,631 of 1,651 roots and 3,218 of 4,635 lemmas have at least one meaning.
- Source 1: English Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0). Root appendix pages (`Appendix:Arabic roots/ن و ر`) and Arabic lemma pages.
- Source 2 (fallback): the most distinctive words of the Saheeh International translation in a root's (or lemma's) verses (TF-IDF style).
- Pure, tested modules in `scripts/lib/glosses/`:
  - `arabic.ts`: root and title spellings, spelling keys, vowel agreement score.
  - `wiki-client.ts`: MediaWiki API client. It sends serial requests with `maxlag=5` and a 6.5 s delay. It retries with backoff and honours `Retry-After`. It uses the generic User-Agent `root-gloss-builder/1.0`.
  - `fetch-plan.ts`, `cache.ts`: decide which pages to fetch, and cache them in `data/raw/wiktionary/` (`appendix.json`, `pages.json`).
  - `wikitext.ts`, `pages.ts`: turn wikitext into short glosses; parse root appendix pages and lemma pages.
  - `match.ts`: match a corpus lemma to a Wiktionary entry.
  - `support.ts`, `fallback.ts`: translation support and fallback words.
  - `glosses.ts`: merge the sources into root and lemma meanings, and count coverage.
- `buildDataset(morph, arabic, english, wiki)` stays pure: `build-data.ts` loads the cache and passes it in.
- Search: `src/search/english.ts` exports `englishMatcher` (registered in `MATCHERS`).
- Root panel: root meanings under the transliteration; the first meaning in each lemma chip. Checked on desktop and Pixel 7 screenshots.
- `public/data/NOTICE.txt` credits Wiktionary.
- Browser test `e2e/english.spec.ts`: "light" gives نور first; patience, sea and day give صبر, بحر, يوم; the رحم panel shows "mercy" and "merciful".

## How to run
- `pnpm data:glosses`: fetch missing Wiktionary pages into `data/raw/wiktionary/` only.
- `pnpm data`: runs the same fetch first (only what is missing), then builds. After the first fetch it runs offline and gives the same output every time.
- To refresh from Wiktionary, delete `data/raw/wiktionary/` and run `pnpm data`.
- A first full fetch takes about 140 requests and about 15 minutes (see the rate limit below).

## How meanings are chosen
- **Lemma.**
  1. Guess page titles from the corpus spelling: plain letters, superscript alef as ا, final ت as ة, ى as ي.
  2. Parse each `==Arabic==` part-of-speech entry: root box, vocalized head (headword template, else `{{ar-pr}}`), sense lines.
  3. Rank entries: same root (or no root named), same letters, closest vowels, same verb/noun class (corpus verbs end in a fatha), a main entry before a "form of" entry.
  4. Among entries with the same spelling, pick the one whose glosses the lemma's verses use most (قَوْم gets "people", not "standing up").
  5. A "plural of" or "verbal noun of" sense borrows the target's glosses (one hop; the targets are fetched in a second round).
  6. Else use the appendix derived terms (`t=`).
  7. Else use the fallback, only for lemmas with 5 or more verses and a word that occurs in 60% of them.
  8. Keep at most 3 meanings.
- **Root.**
  1. Take the appendix meanings without "related to", "forms terms related to" or "the number".
  2. Keep a meaning only when a lemma shares it or 1% of the root's verses use it. Put the most used first.
  3. Add the top lemma's first meaning.
  4. Else use the first meanings of the top lemmas.
  5. Else use the fallback: a word in 20% of the verses that is 3 times more frequent there than overall.
  6. Keep at most 4 meanings.
- **Gloss cleaning.**
  - Links and glossing templates become text. Labels, qualifiers, notes, quotes and parentheses are removed.
  - Text is split on `, ; : /`.
  - Phrases are dropped when they are over 4 words (3 for later phrases and root meanings), or are "etc", "cf. …", "forms …", or a fragment that ends in a preposition.
  - Senses labelled dialect, colloquial, modern and similar are skipped.
  - Meanings are lowercase except proper names (proper-noun entries, multi-word capitalized phrases).
  - Duplicates are removed by English stem (day/days, sky/skies).

## Search
`englishMatcher` scores:
- The whole meaning equals the query: 1 (`TIER.exact`).
- The query is a run of whole words inside a meaning: 0.99.
- Stem match (lights, merciful/mercy, patient/patience): `TIER.prefix`.
- Substring of 3 or more letters: `TIER.contains`.
- Lemma meanings score 0.02 lower than root meanings.
- Arabic queries return []. The reason is `{ kind: 'english', text: <meaning> }`.

## Decisions
- **No category listing.** The first plan listed `Category:Arabic terms belonging to the root …` for every root (1,651 requests). Wikimedia now allows only 10 requests a minute for clients without contact details in the User-Agent, and the brief forbids contact details. So the fetch guesses titles from the lemma spelling (50 titles per request) and stays at about 9 requests a minute. The cost: lemmas whose Wiktionary page has a different spelling are missed.
- **Translation support.** Saheeh International words choose between homographs and filter appendix senses that the Quran does not use (أرض "coryza", عذب "particles of rubbish"). Every Wiktionary-sourced gloss is still Wiktionary text; the translation only selects.
- **Root first.** Root meanings from the appendix come before lemma meanings (نور: light, fire).

## Open issues
- Some meanings are wrong senses: ربب "many a man" (from رُبَّ), أَرَيْ "honey", وُقِفُ "bracelet", شُحُوم "gagea reticulata", تَذْكِير "masculine gender", وقف "bracelet". Corpus lemmas that are inflected forms match the wrong page or none.
- Some root meanings come from weak fallback words (ركد "everyone").
- Verb meanings keep "to" (to say, to know). Some adjectives get a long gloss (عَلِيم "having great knowledge").
- A full refetch is slow (about 15 minutes) because of the 10 requests a minute limit. An identified User-Agent with contact details would get 200 a minute.
- `src/search/matchers.ts`: the change adds one import line and one item in the `MATCHERS` list. G7 edits the same list, so expect a trivial merge.
